# Composable capacity — discovery hypotheses

**Status:** evidence-seeking  
**Updated:** 2026-08-25

This document translates the current operator evidence into testable product
hypotheses. It does not declare a validated market or grant one interview the
weight of repeated buyer evidence.

## H1 — Capability-first planning

Operators begin with a required operational output and translate it into sensor,
payload, endurance, range, relay, or other capability requirements before
selecting a compatible platform.

## H2 — Heterogeneous composition

Some objectives are better served by composing several different capabilities
than by selecting one nominally interchangeable vehicle.

## H3 — Dynamic composition

The useful composition changes when an executor fails, a capability disappears,
capacity returns, or priorities change.

## H4 — Delegated mission authority

Mission/risk owners will let SwarmOS make named decision kinds automatically
inside a mission-scoped, revisioned authority grant. Test launch composition,
failed-executor replacement, and capacity reinforcement separately; do not ask
about “autonomy” as one undifferentiated choice.

### H4a — Progressive trust

Operators may start with launch review while delegating bounded replacement or
reinforcement, then widen the grant only after observing reliable outcomes.
SWARM must not calculate or expand this trust automatically in the current
product.

### H4b — Explainable allocation

Adoption and delegation improve when the operator can inspect requirements,
constraints, candidates, exclusions, selected roles, and the exact grant
revision behind each recommendation.

## Conditions, not independent top-level hypotheses yet

- **H5 — Integration feasibility:** existing fleet, identity, airspace, and
  safety systems can supply the facts needed for the decision boundary.
- **H6 — Economic advantage:** faster decisions, higher utilization, lower
  interruption cost, or resilience create enough value to buy the system.

H5 and H6 remain adoption conditions under H4 until repeated buyer evidence
justifies treating them as independently validated hypotheses.

## Evidence rule

For each interview retain the operator's exact language and distinguish:

- confirmed current workflow;
- stated delegable and non-delegable decisions;
- required rationale/evidence;
- constraints named by the mission/risk owner;
- what remains unknown, including willingness to pay.
